# House style guide

## Structure
- Situation Recap opens with one sentence identifying the borrower (business, scale, sponsor) before moving to the causes of deterioration.
- Recent Development is ordered: legal status / court events -> plan treatment of claims -> provision movement and rationale -> outlook / next milestone.

## Phrasing
- Refer to the bank as "the Bank"; to the borrower by its short name after first mention.
- Money: "$45.0mm", "$1.42bn". Leverage: "19.3x". Dates: "August 4, 2026" or "Sep-26" for provision periods.
- Provision movements: "Provision increased from **$18.0mm** to **$29.3mm** (65% of exposure), driven by ..." — always state from/to and the driver.
- Plan treatment: name the class, then what it receives, then "pro-rata" where applicable.

## Bolding
- Bold: provision amounts and coverage, Chapter 11 filing date, recovery %, EV, the Bank's exposure.
- Do not bold: company names, generic phrases, more than 3 items per point.

## Avoid
- Hedging adjectives ("significantly", "very"), restating the memo's headings, recommendations not in the memo.
