## Company Overview
[OV-1] Velora Fitness Holdings LLC ("Velora") operates 212 mid-tier fitness clubs in the U.S. Midwest and Southeast under the Velora and FlexFit brands. FY2025 revenue was $610mm with Adj. EBITDA of $48mm. The Company was acquired by Summit Bridge Equity in 2020.
### Capital Structure
[OV-2] Funded debt of $780mm comprises a $75mm super-senior RCF (fully drawn), a $525mm 1st Lien Term Loan due 2027 and $180mm of 2nd Lien Term Loan. Leverage was 16.3x at FY2025. The Bank holds $30.0mm of the 1st Lien Term Loan.

## Reason for Rating
### Membership Trends
[RR-1] Membership declined 17% from the 2022 peak as low-cost competitors expanded into Velora's core markets, while connected home-fitness products reduced demand for mid-tier clubs.
[RR-2] Club-level costs increased sharply, with utilities and wages up 11% and 9% respectively in FY2025, compressing Adj. EBITDA margin from 15.1% in FY2022 to 7.9% in FY2025.
### Restructuring Path
[RR-3] After failing to refinance the 2027 maturity, the Company hired restructuring advisors in November 2025 and missed its January 2026 interest payment on the 1st Lien Term Loan. The credit was downgraded to Doubtful in February 2026.

## Transactional Updates
[TU-1] On May 12, 2026, Velora filed for Chapter 11 in the Southern District of Texas with a prepackaged plan supported by 91% of 1st Lien lenders and 67% of 2nd Lien lenders.
[TU-2] Under the plan, 1st Lien lenders receive pro-rata (i) a $200mm take-back 1st Lien Term Loan and (ii) 92% of reorganized equity. 2nd Lien lenders receive 8% of reorganized equity. The RCF is refinanced in full by an exit facility. 78 underperforming leases will be rejected.
[TU-3] The plan was confirmed on June 24, 2026, and the Company emerged on July 15, 2026.

## Provision Rationale & Accounting Impact
[PR-1] The provision was increased from $12.0mm to $16.5mm (55% of exposure) in June 2026, reflecting the plan valuation of $310mm EV, below the $380mm assumed at downgrade.
[PR-2] Upon emergence, the Bank received $11.4mm of take-back Term Loan and 5.3% of reorganized equity (fair value $2.1mm). A charge-off of $16.5mm was taken against the provision; no further P&L impact.

## Outlook and Action Plan
[OL-1] The take-back Term Loan will be rated Substandard and monitored quarterly. The equity position has been transferred to the Equity Holdings Group.
