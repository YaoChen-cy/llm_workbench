**Situation Recap**

- Velora is a Summit Bridge-sponsored operator of 212 mid-tier fitness clubs (FY2025 revenue $610mm). Membership fell **17% from peak** as low-cost competitors and home-fitness products eroded demand, while wage and utility inflation compressed Adj. EBITDA margin from 15.1% to **7.9%**.
- Cap structure of **$780mm** (super-senior RCF, $525mm 1st Lien TL, $180mm 2nd Lien TL) left leverage at **16.3x**; the Bank holds **$30.0mm** of the 1st Lien TL.
- Unable to refinance the 2027 maturity, the Company engaged advisors, missed the January 2026 1st Lien interest payment and was downgraded to **Doubtful** in February 2026.

**Recent Development**

- Velora filed a prepackaged **Chapter 11 on May 12, 2026** (91% 1st Lien / 67% 2nd Lien support); the plan was confirmed June 24 and the Company **emerged July 15, 2026**.
- 1st Lien lenders received pro-rata a **$200mm take-back TL** and **92% of reorganized equity**; 2nd Lien received 8% of equity; the RCF was refinanced in full by the exit facility.
- Provision increased from $12.0mm to **$16.5mm (55%)** in June 2026, driven by plan EV of **$310mm** vs. $380mm assumed at downgrade. On emergence the Bank received $11.4mm of take-back TL and 5.3% of equity; **$16.5mm was charged off** against the provision with no further P&L impact.
