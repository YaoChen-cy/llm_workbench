**Situation Recap**

- Harborline is a Crestmark-owned furniture designer and omni-channel retailer (118 showrooms, FY2025 revenue $1.42bn). The housing downturn drove same-store sales down **14% in FY2025**, while tariffs and a failed ERP migration compressed Adj. EBITDA margin to **5.0%**.
- Funded debt of **$1.35bn** ($850mm 1st Lien TLB, $360mm 2nd Lien Notes, ABL) left leverage at **19.3x**; the Bank holds **$45.0mm** of the 1st Lien TLB.
- Following a failed turnaround, the Company engaged advisors, skipped the March 2026 2nd Lien coupon and was downgraded to **Doubtful** in April 2026.

**Recent Development**

- Harborline filed **Chapter 11 on August 4, 2026** with an RSA backed by ~78% of 1st Lien lenders (incl. the Bank); a $150mm DIP with a $50mm roll-up was approved, without Bank participation.
- 1st Lien lenders receive pro-rata a **$150mm Exit TL** and **85% of reorganized equity** (pre-MIP); 2nd Lien receives 15% plus warrants; GUCs face cramdown if they reject.
- Provision increased from $18.0mm to **$29.3mm (65%)** in September 2026, driven by the Disclosure Statement EV of **$420mm** vs. $600mm previously assumed. Estimated Bank recovery is **$15.7mm**; DS hearing on October 28, 2026.
